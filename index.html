The gap

Justification may be settled. Sanctification still needs training. — 1 Timothy 4:7 · James 1:22 (NLT)

The practice

Five steps for the moment it costs something

Small enough to run before you speak. Drawn from ordinary biblical commands — not a second gospel, and not a path to emotional emptiness.

01

Notice

Pause. Name what is rising. It is not the whole of who you are in Christ.

“Something is rising — I see it.”

02

Discern

Hold Galatians 5’s two lists against what rose: sinful nature, or fruit of the Spirit?

“Which list does this belong to?”

03

Declare

Speak a specific Scripture you already hid in your heart into this exact moment.

Anger · Anxiety · Envy · Bitterness — name the true word aloud.

04

Return

A brief prayer now. Peace follows prayer; it does not come first.

“Lord, rule here. Help me love them.”

05

Act

Obey the word now. Do not wait for the feeling to leave before you move.

Do the next faithful thing while it still costs something.
