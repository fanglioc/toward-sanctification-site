Practice Handout: The Five Steps

From Justification toward Sanctification
A Practice for Living What You Already Believe · NLT

The gap

We often know the right thing — and still react from old habits. Justification may be settled; sanctification still needs training (1 Timothy 4:7).

The Five Steps

Step What you do One-line cue
1. Notice Pause. Name what is rising. It is not the whole of who you are in Christ. “Something is rising — I see it.”
2. Discern Galatians 5 — sinful nature or fruit of the Spirit? “This is my sinful nature, not who I am becoming in Christ.”
3. Declare Speak a specific Scripture into the moment. Anger: Eph 4:26 · Anxiety / phone: Phil 4:6 · Bitterness: Eph 4:31–32
4. Return Brief prayer — turn to God now. “Lord, rule here. Help me love them.”
5. Act Obey the word now — don’t wait for the feeling to leave. Do the next faithful thing.

Galatians 5 quick check

Sinful nature includes: hostility, quarreling, jealousy, outbursts of anger, envy…

Fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control.

Goal: fruit of the Spirit in real relationships — not emotional emptiness.

Live practice (60 seconds)

Think of one trigger you face often (especially at home). Silently run the five steps once right now.

One-week challenge

  1. Pick one recurring trigger.
  2. Practice the five steps every time it appears.
  3. Report to a friend, spouse, or small group.

“Don’t just listen to God’s word. You must do what it says.” — James 1:22 (NLT)

We train because we have been justified — not in order to be justified.
Justified once. Sanctified daily.
