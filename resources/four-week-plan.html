Four-Week Practice Plan

From Justification toward Sanctification · Personal, couple, or small-group use · NLT
Isolate one or two skills each week. Repeat a week if unfinished. Goal: fruit of the Spirit in a real relationship — not a private technique.

Week 1 — Notice and Observe

Focus: See a rising response as a discrete event before deciding anything about it.

  • Daily: Three “Notice” reminders. Ask: What is rising in me right now?
  • Trigger: One recurring situation. Pause three seconds; name what arrives. Do not fix it yet.
  • Evening: Three lines — what rose, when, how you responded.
  • Carry: James 1:22. Community: Tell one person in one sentence; ask them to check on Sunday.

Week 2 — Discern and Declare

Focus: Which list? Then speak truth into the moment.

  • Daily: Keep Notice reminders. Read Galatians 5:19–23 morning and night.
  • Trigger: After noticing, ask which list it resembles. Declare one true sentence or verse aloud (even silently spoken counts).
  • Evening: Note which list “won” — look for patterns, not a grade.
  • Carry: Galatians 5:17 or Ephesians 4:26. Community: Report the pattern in facts, not excuses.

Week 3 — Return and Act

Focus: Complete the sequence: pray briefly, then obey before the feeling catches up.

  • Daily: Morning prayer naming a person or situation. Keep Notice reminders.
  • Trigger: Run all five steps. Log date, situation, steps completed, outcome.
  • Evening: Which step was hardest today — and why?
  • Carry: Philippians 4:6–7 (prayer first, peace after). Community: Midweek check-in text.

Week 4 — Habit and Community

Focus: Weave the five steps into a rhythm sturdy enough for a bad week.

  • Daily: Morning orientation · moment-by-moment return · evening examination (Psalm 139:23–24).
  • Trigger: Keep the first; optionally add a second without abandoning the first.
  • Evening: One honest paragraph: what changed, what did not, why you continue.
  • Carry: Romans 12:2. Community: Share the real log; set a ninety-day check-in date in writing.

Starter verses to hide before the crisis: Galatians 5:22–23 · Romans 12:2 · Psalm 119:11 · Philippians 4:6–7
Justified once. Sanctified daily.
