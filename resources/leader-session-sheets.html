Leader Session Sheets

From Justification toward Sanctification · ~75 minutes · Use with Appendix B homework · NLT

Weekly half-page homework

My one trigger: _______________   Steps: Notice · Discern · Declare · Return · Act

Evening (3 lines): What rose (and when)? What did I do? One thing for tomorrow?

Report: one honest success or failure. Watch-out: colder home = wrong goal.

Session 1 — Gap

10' James 1:23–25 + silence · 25' 1–2 discussion Qs · 10' whiteboard gap + pocket card · 15' practice preview · 10' pray · 5' assign Start Tonight / B Week 1. Required: Ch. 1 or Start Tonight.

Session 2 — Diagnoses

10' four diagnoses on board · 25' hearing≠growing + justification/sanctification · 10' grace trains ≠ earning · 15' “What will I do differently?” · pray · close. Split 2a/2b or assign Ch. 6 only.

Session 3 — Inner war

Gal 5:17 twice · two lists · guardrail: fruit not emptiness · practice report · Ps 139. Required: Gal 5:16–25.

Session 4 — Five steps live

Whiteboard steps · separate Declare vs Return (60 sec) · anger script / role-play · personal cue verse · pray triggers · pocket cards. Required: Ch. 10 + card. Skip Ch. 9 if needed.

Session 5 — Habit & FAQs

Evening-exam hands · one habit Q + Ch. 12 “feel nothing?” · Heb 10:24–25 · three-line limit · pray homes · draft one rhythm sentence.

Session 6 — Becoming

Ordinary cross · retreat vs rhythm · ninety-day sentence each · what changed since week 1 · named prayer · write check-in date before leaving.

You don’t need Greek. You need a pocket card, a practice report, and the courage to stop theory without a trigger.
