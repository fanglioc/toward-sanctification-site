## The five steps

When something rises in you — before you speak or act:

1. **Notice** — Pause. Name what is rising. It is not the whole of who you are in Christ.
2. **Discern** — Sinful nature, or fruit of the Spirit? (Galatians 5)
3. **Declare** — Speak God’s word into this exact moment.
4. **Return** — A brief prayer. Peace follows; it need not arrive first.
5. **Act** — Obey now. Do not wait for the feeling.

## About the book

*From Justification toward Sanctification* is a discipleship book on why sincere believers often know so much and change so little — and how to train sanctification after justification in ordinary moments.

This repository is the public home for the free ebook and practice resources.
